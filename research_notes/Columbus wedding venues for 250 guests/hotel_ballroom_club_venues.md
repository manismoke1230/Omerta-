# Hotel Ballrooms, Banquet Halls & Country/Golf Club Wedding Venues in the Columbus Metro (250 seated guests)

Research date: 2026-11-26. Scope: hotels, ballrooms/banquet/conference centers, and country/golf clubs in Franklin, Delaware, Licking, Fairfield, Union, Madison, and Pickaway counties. Barns/estates, museums/gardens/landmarks, and downtown loft/industrial spaces are excluded (other researchers cover them).

Sourcing notes for the report writer:
- **The Knot blocked automated access (HTTP 403).** Knot ratings, review counts, and Best of Weddings years therefore mostly come secondhand from search snippets. WeddingWire (WW) venue pages, Zola, Wedding-Spot, Cvent, and venue PDFs were fetched directly.
- **WW "starting price" and "site fee" figures are vendor-entered and undated.** WW pages are labeled "2026", but the reviews on many of them are years old.
- **Many venue-published package PDFs found are from 2020–2021.** They are flagged throughout as "pre-2024 / possibly outdated".
- **Capacity numbers on aggregators are often theater or standing counts.** The figures below use "seated / with dance floor" wherever a source says so.

## Q1. Maximum seated capacity with a dance floor. Does the best room comfortably fit 250 seated?

### Takeaway
**Comfortably fit 250 seated with a dance floor (ballroom of 300+ seated or larger):**
- *Downtown hotels:* Hilton Columbus Downtown, Hyatt Regency, Renaissance Downtown, Westin Great Southern (Grand Ballroom 300 seated, the smallest of this group), The Plaza Hotel at Capitol Square
- *Suburban hotels:* Hilton Easton, Hilton Polaris, Marriott Northwest (Dublin), Marriott University Area (OSU), Renaissance Westerville–Polaris, Embassy Suites Dublin, Crowne Plaza North–Worthington, The Ohioan (ex-Nationwide Hotel & Conference Center)
- *Licking County:* Cherry Valley Hotel (Newark)
- *Banquet and event centers:* Villa Milano, Valley Dale Ballroom, Bridgewater Banquet Center, Makoy Center, Aladdin Shrine Center, Ohio Union, The Exchange at Bridge Park
- *Clubs:* Brookside G&CC, Pinnacle GC, The Medallion Club, Jefferson G&CC, Wedgewood G&CC

**At-limit squeezes (listed max is about 250–275 seated):** Scioto Reserve CC, Club at Corazon, Worthington Hills CC, The Lakes G&CC, Golf Club at Little Turtle, Royal American Links, Grand Event Center, Sonesta Columbus Downtown.

**Too small or tight (200–240 seated):** The Blackwell Inn, Fawcett Center, Heritage GC (Banquet Hall 225), Columbus Country Club (225, and only with a member sponsor), The Wenbeck, The Junto, Le Méridien The Joseph, Ivory Room, Tartan Fields (ballroom; its tent holds more).

### Inferences
- **The 250-seat threshold eliminates most "boutique" hotels** (Junto, Le Méridien, Canopy, LeVeque, Graduate) and several marquee private clubs (Muirfield Village, Golf Club of Dublin, Columbus CC).
- **Large convention-style ballrooms are oversized for 250.** This applies to Hilton Downtown, Hyatt, Renaissance, Hilton Easton, Hilton Polaris, and the Ohio Union full ballroom. These hotels will typically airwall a section, so ask which subdivided room is offered. A half-empty ballroom can feel cavernous.
- **The Westin's Grand Ballroom (300 seated) is about the "right-size" downtown room for 250 plus a dance floor.** Brookside (275 with dance floor), Medallion (330), Wedgewood (300), and The Ohioan's Grand Ballroom (300 wedding / 432 rounds) are similar right-sized suburban options.
- **For the at-limit venues, ask for a floor plan with 25–26 rounds of 10 plus a dance floor and DJ/band footprint before signing.** This covers Scioto Reserve, Corazon, Worthington Hills, Lakes, Little Turtle, Royal American Links, and Grand Event Center.

## Q2. Can the ceremony be held on site (separate space or outdoor vs. room flip)? Rain plan?

### Takeaway
**Ceremony fees and spaces.** Nearly every venue in scope offers on-site ceremonies for a separate fee, typically $500–$1,500 and up to $4,000 at the Westin.
- **Clubs** generally offer an outdoor lawn or garden with the ballroom or a patio as the rain plan.
- **Downtown hotels** offer a separate indoor ballroom, or a terrace at the Renaissance and Plaza.
- **Dedicated outdoor ceremony sites:** The Ohioan (new 2025 courtyard), the Blackwell (terrace), and the Exchange (courtyard, tent and permit extra).
- **Room flips:** Pinnacle and Villa Milano flip the room. Valley Dale's ceremony arrangements are undocumented.
