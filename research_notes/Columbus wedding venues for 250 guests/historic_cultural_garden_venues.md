# Historic, Cultural, Museum, Garden and Landmark Wedding Venues in Greater Columbus, Ohio, for a 250-Guest Seated Reception (researched Nov 26, 2026)

Scope note: this file covers historic buildings, museums, gardens and conservatories, theaters, the zoo, parks, and university and civic landmarks within about 45 minutes of downtown Columbus. Other researchers cover hotels and ballrooms, country clubs, barns and rustic estates, and downtown loft or industrial spaces. Prices are listed with their source year. Anything older than 2024 is flagged "possibly outdated." Several venue sites (ohiohistory.org, theknot.com, nbc4i.com) blocked automated fetching, so some figures for those come from search-engine snippets and are marked that way.

Shortlist at a glance: Ohio Statehouse, COSI, Ohio Union, Columbus Museum of Art, Columbus Zoo (Africa Event Center), Franklin Park Conservatory (Wells Barn campus), Boat House at Confluence Park, Bryn Du Mansion. Ohio History Center is uncertain. The Columbus Athenaeum is closed.

---

## Q1. Maximum SEATED capacity with a dance floor: which venues really fit 250 seated?

### Takeaway
Only a few venues in this category clearly seat 250 for dinner and still leave room to dance. The roomiest are Ohio Union (Grand Ballroom or East Ballroom), COSI (galleries are listed "up to 300", with tables and chairs for 300), the Boat House at Confluence Park (Buckeye Room 350–425 seated), and the Ohio Statehouse (275 cap for weddings). Columbus Museum of Art (Pavilion), the Columbus Zoo (Africa Event Center) and Franklin Park Conservatory (Wells Barn campus) each list exactly 250 for a seated dinner, so 250 plus a dance floor is a squeeze at those three. Several well-known names fall below 250: the FPC Palm House is closed until fall 2027, and Jeffrey Mansion, CAPA theater rooms, the Huntington Club, the Faculty Club and Longaberger Alumni House are all too small.

### Inferences
- **Comparison grid** (synthesized from the findings above; "fit" assumes 250 seated at rounds plus a dance floor):

| Venue | Best room for 250 | Listed seated max | Fit for 250 + dance floor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ohio Union | Grand or East Ballroom | 980 / 600 banquet (860 / 560 in older guide) | Comfortable (East Ballroom is the right size) |
| COSI | Gallery 1 or 2 (or tented English Plaza) | 300 per gallery | Comfortable to slightly snug |
| Boat House at Confluence Park | Buckeye Room | 350 (Zola) to 425–500 | Comfortable |

| Columbus Zoo | Africa Event Center | 250 (Zola) / 400 (Tripleseat) | **Tight to uncertain**: confirm floor plan |

- At venues where "250" is the published seated-dinner maximum (CMA, Zoo, FPC), that figure usually means rounds without a full dance floor plus band and buffet footprint. The planner should ask each venue for a 250-guest floor plan with a dance floor.

### Gaps
- No venue publishes a "seated with dance floor" figure. Every number above is a generic seated or banquet figure.
- I could not open COSI's or the Zoo's official wedding capacity charts. The Zoo's official venues page lists no capacities.
- I could not access the Ohio History Connection's official rental page (Cloudflare block), so OHC capacities come from third parties.
- Bryn Du's official site gives no seated count for the Field House.

## Q2. Can the ceremony be held on site (separate space or room flip)? Indoor/outdoor options and rain plan?

### Takeaway
Most shortlisted venues host the ceremony on site in a separate space, so no room flip is needed: Statehouse (Rotunda/Atrium), COSI (Planetarium or English Plaza), CMA (Edwards Court, Sculpture Garden or Pavilion), the Zoo, the Boat House (Gazebo or deck), Bryn Du (gardens and lawn), and FPC (garden spaces). Outdoor options at CMA, FPC, Bryn Du, the Zoo and the Boat House need a written rain plan.

### Cited Findings
- **Ohio Statehouse**:
  - The $10,000 package covers ceremony and reception and includes a rehearsal and two wedding suites — [Statehouse FAQ](https://www.ohiostatehouse.org/Assets/Files/1003285.pdf).
  - The South Porch off the Atrium is available for cocktail hour only if both ceremony and reception are at the Statehouse. Heat or weather protection must come from an approved provider — [Statehouse FAQ](https://www.ohiostatehouse.org/Assets/Files/1003285.pdf).
  - The weddings page's videos reference a Rotunda ceremony and an Atrium reception. The package lists the State Room and Ladies Gallery — [Ohio Statehouse Weddings](https://www.ohiostatehouse.org/events/weddings).
- **COSI**: Planetarium ceremony with custom night-sky show, $1,600. English Plaza ceremony (outdoor), $900. One-hour cocktail hour in a classic exhibit, $550 — [WeddingWire COSI](https://www.weddingwire.com/biz/cosi-columbus/92b66fa31c79a67e.html).
- **Columbus Museum of Art**:
  - Ceremony options are Edwards Court (200), the Sculpture Garden (250) or the Pavilion (350).
  - The Sculpture Garden is weather-dependent and needs an approved rain plan.
  - Evening rentals include exclusive use of Edwards Court, Broad Street Lobby, Pavilion, Atrium and Sculpture Garden, 5pm–midnight.
  - A complimentary one-hour rehearsal is available during museum hours.

  Source: [CMA Weddings](https://www.columbusmuseum.org/weddings).
- **Columbus Zoo**:
  - Outdoor and indoor ceremonies are offered — [Columbus Zoo Weddings](https://www.columbuszoo.org/weddings).
  - The $500 on-site ceremony fee applies at the Africa Event Center or Water's Edge — [Zola Columbus Zoo](https://www.zola.com/wedding-vendors/wedding-venues/columbus-zoo-and-aquarium).
  - Water's Edge pavilions are heated and their walls open or close — [WeddingWire Columbus Zoo](https://www.weddingwire.com/biz/columbus-zoo-and-aquarium-powell/99161911e8ca571c.html).
- **Franklin Park Conservatory**:
  - The ceremony fee is $1,200 and includes dressing suites and a one-hour rehearsal (scheduled Mon–Fri 9am–4pm) — [HCTG FPC](https://www.herecomestheguide.com/wedding-venues/ohio/franklin-park-conservatory-and-botanical-gardens).
  - Indoor and outdoor ceremony capacity is 250 each — [HCTG FPC](https://www.herecomestheguide.com/wedding-venues/ohio/franklin-park-conservatory-and-botanical-gardens).
  - A 2019 reviewer was forced off the outdoor terrace by rain — [WeddingWire FPC](https://www.weddingwire.com/biz/franklin-park-conservatory-and-botanical-gardens-columbus/7b7efb8df8874f52.html).
- **Boat House at Confluence Park**: Gazebo ceremony (up to 225 seated), $1,250. The Buckeye Room is also listed for ceremonies. A reviewer held the ceremony on the deck overlooking the rivers, which has a handicap elevator — [Zola Boat House](https://zola.com/wedding-vendors/wedding-venues/the-boat-house-at-confluence-park).
- **Bryn Du Mansion**:
  - Ceremony sites are the Formal Garden, Front Patio, Great Lawn and Field House Terrace — [Bryn Du Weddings](https://www.bryndu.com/weddings).
  - A reviewer said staff tracked the weather and moved a ceremony indoors — [WeddingWire Bryn Du](https://www.weddingwire.com/biz/bryn-du-mansion-granville/de5f0c19d9de1d4e.html).
- **Ohio Union**: Performance Hall connects to Potter Plaza, a private outdoor space (guide circa 2018–2020) — [Ohio Union Wedding Guide](https://ohiounion.osu.edu/posts/documents/o-h-i-do-wedding-guide.pdf).
- **Ohio History Center**: The Knot describes a package with a rehearsal dinner in the Victorian Room and four hours on the Plaza Terrace for the reception (per search snippet) — [The Knot OHC](https://www.theknot.com/marketplace/ohio-history-center-columbus-oh-2048705).

### Inferences
- Indoor ceremony with a guaranteed separate reception room:
  - Strongest: Statehouse (Rotunda to Atrium), COSI (Planetarium to Gallery), CMA (Edwards Court to Pavilion).
  - Also workable: Ohio Union (Performance Hall to East Ballroom) and the Zoo (Water's Edge to Africa Event Center). These are inferences from their multiple separate rooms, not stated package combinations.
- At FPC, the Palm House is closed until fall 2027. A 250-guest ceremony will therefore likely be outdoors on the Celebration Lawn or in the Garden Pavilion (300 theater). The rain plan for 250 is a key question to ask.

### Gaps
- Exact rain-plan policy (tent requirement, cut-off times, extra fees) was not published for FPC, the Zoo, the Boat House or Bryn Du.
- I found no confirmation of whether COSI's Planetarium seats 250 for a ceremony.

## Q3. Pricing: rental fees, F&B minimums, per-person packages, peak vs off-peak, typical totals for 250

### Takeaway
Venue fees run from about $1,500 (Boat House room) to $15,000 (CMA peak reception site fee). The Statehouse charges a flat $10,000 with no discounts. COSI and the Boat House publish all-in per-person packages: COSI $140–165 per person, Boat House $50–94 per person. Many published figures are third-party and undated, so quotes need confirming.

### Cited Findings
- **Ohio Statehouse**:
  - Statehouse Wedding Package: $10,000. Includes rehearsal, two wedding suites, white padded chairs, tables, AV, parking, security (two Ohio Highway Patrol troopers) and staffing. Catering and bar are extra.
  - "The Ohio Statehouse is a government building and cannot offer any discounts, for any reason."
  - Payment: 10% deposit with the permit packet; full payment due 30 days out.
  - Cancellation: 50% of the deposit is refunded up to 90 days out; nothing under 30 days.

  Source (May 2025): [Statehouse FAQ](https://www.ohiostatehouse.org/Assets/Files/1003285.pdf); confirmed $10,000 on [Ohio Statehouse Weddings](https://www.ohiostatehouse.org/events/weddings) (2026 page). Older third-party split pricing ($3,500 ceremony / $4,500 reception / $8,000 both) is about 6 years old and outdated — [Style Story Creative](https://www.stylestorycreative.com/the-ohio-statehouse/).
- **COSI** (WeddingWire listing; package dates not shown; reviews date from 2009–2018, so **possibly outdated**):
  - Celestial Package, $140 per person: 4.5-hour house bar, 2 passed hors d'oeuvres plus crudité, 2-entrée buffet, tables and chairs for up to 300, linens, access to 2 exhibits, dressing suite, tasting for 4, coordinator.
  - Galactic Package, $165 per person: premium bar, 3 hors d'oeuvres, plated duo-entrée dinner, upgraded linens.
  - Space fees: Gallery 1 or 2 reception (4 hours, up to 300) $3,400; English Plaza reception (up to 600) $3,600; Atrium (up to 150) $2,800.
  - Starting site fee $550 (same peak and off-peak). Bar from $21 per person.
  - Peak months: June, July, August, October.

  Source: [WeddingWire COSI](https://www.weddingwire.com/biz/cosi-columbus/92b66fa31c79a67e.html). Wedding Spot shows "starting at $8,750 for 50 guests" with valet parking — [Wedding Spot COSI](https://www.wedding-spot.com/venue/12994/cosi/).

  - WeddingWire: starting price $5,000. Reception site fee $15,000 peak (Apr–Dec) and $1,250 off-peak (Jan–Mar). Ceremony site fee $5,000. Bar from $45 per person — [WeddingWire CMA](https://www.weddingwire.com/biz/columbus-museum-of-art-columbus/1b8802523da9c703.html).

## Q7. Pros, cons, restrictions, recurring complaints

### Takeaway
The biggest constraints are the Statehouse's strict décor list and hard 11pm stop, CMA's peak pricing and history of caterer complaints, the FPC Palm House closure, COSI's child-focused image and post-event handling complaints, and day-only or ceremony-only rules at arboretums and city parks.

### Cited Findings
- **Ohio Statehouse**:
  - Hard end time: "11pm is the end time to all weddings. Going past 11pm is prohibited. Lights go on at 11pm," with teardown 11pm–midnight.
  - Banned items: adhesives and tape, balloons, birdseed or rice, bubbles, candles without glass containers, smoking or vaping, confetti, glitter, loose fresh flower petals, signs on stakes, straw or hay, sparklers or fireworks.
  - Vendor setup starts only 2–3 hours before.
  - Airport-style security screening by Ohio Highway Patrol for every guest.

  Source: [Statehouse FAQ](https://www.ohiostatehouse.org/Assets/Files/1003285.pdf).
- **Columbus Museum of Art**:
  - Pros: exclusive building use 5pm–midnight; access to Galleries 1–10; private security; a dedicated event manager.
  - Restrictions: the Sculpture Garden cannot be rented during public hours. Thursday elopements share the building with the public.

  Source: [CMA Weddings](https://www.columbusmuseum.org/weddings).
  - Complaints: high catering costs and limited caterer choice (2013 and 2017 reviews); coordinator turnover and poor communication (2017). Response time is the lowest sub-score at 4.2 — [WeddingWire CMA](https://www.weddingwire.com/biz/columbus-museum-of-art-columbus/1b8802523da9c703.html).
- **COSI**:
  - Pros: Planetarium ceremony, skyline views, exhibit photos.

  Source: [WeddingWire COSI](https://www.weddingwire.com/biz/cosi-columbus/92b66fa31c79a67e.html).
