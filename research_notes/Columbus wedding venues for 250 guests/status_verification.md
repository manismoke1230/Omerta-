## 1. Columbus Athenaeum (32 N 4th St): Is it booking/hosting weddings 2026–2027? Sold? Who operates events? Cancellations?

### Takeaway
Treat the Athenaeum as **not a viable 2027 venue**. It has been in receivership since October 2025 and was listed for sale at $8.5M in March 2026. I found no report of a sale. The official domain now redirects to a concert promoter's page that lists **no upcoming events at the Athenaeum** (checked Nov 26, 2026). I found no reporting that wedding bookings were cancelled, but nothing shows active wedding booking either. Confidence: high that it is not a safe booking; medium on sale status, because I could not reach county court records.

### Cited Findings
- **Listing (March 2026):** The Robert Weiler Company listed the building at 32 N. Fourth St. for $8,500,000. The property fell into foreclosure in September 2025, and a receiver was appointed in October 2025 "to oversee the building and its operations." The listing requires proof of funds and a substantial deposit. It covers a ballroom for 1,000+ guests, a theater, a banquet room, a restaurant with full kitchen, and some furniture, fixtures and equipment — [NBC4](https://www.nbc4i.com/news/local-news/columbus/columbus-athenaeum-listed-for-8-5-million-amid-receivership/); [Yahoo syndication](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/columbus-athenaeum-listed-8-5-130000510.html); [Columbus Underground, "Unique Properties for Sale"](https://columbusunderground.com/unique-properties-for-sale-the-athenaeum-we1/)
- Columbus Underground gives the asking price as $8.5M ($53/sq ft) — [Columbus Underground](https://columbusunderground.com/unique-properties-for-sale-the-athenaeum-we1/)
- **No sale found:** Two separate searches (Nov 2026) found no news of a buyer, a court-approved sale, or a price change after March 2026 — [NBC4](https://www.nbc4i.com/news/local-news/columbus/columbus-athenaeum-listed-for-8-5-million-amid-receivership/); [Columbus Business Beat (Yahoo Finance)](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/columbus-business-beat-historic-athenaeum-013000719.html)
- **"New ownership" headline is old:** The Yahoo/Dispatch story "Athenaeum theater Downtown falls under new ownership" is dated **Jan 23, 2024**. It covers the TempleLive (Beaty Capital Group, Arkansas) acquisition and says TempleLive planned to "continue to operate the venue for private events." It is not a 2026 sale — [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/news/athenaeum-theater-downtown-falls-under-130000349.html)
- **Sept 2025 shutdown:** TempleLive shut all five of its venues at once in September 2025 and cancelled Athenaeum concerts, with Ticketmaster refunds. Shows booked by Celebrity Etc Presents were initially still on — [Columbus Underground](https://columbusunderground.com/athenaeum-owner-cancels-all-shows-across-all-venues-we1/); [The Lantern, Sept 2025](https://www.thelantern.com/2025/09/historical-downtown-theater-cancels-several-live-events-through-december/); [Columbus Navigator](https://www.columbusnavigator.com/historic-columbus-venue-sees-concerts-axed-as-operator-pulls-out/); [Urban One TV](https://urbanonetvcolumbus.com/869557/all-shows-at-the-columbus-athenaeum-canceled/)
- A cancellation-period article (Sept 2025) said people could "still" rent the venue for private events, including weddings, through its website. That statement is more than a year old — [Columbus Navigator](https://www.columbusnavigator.com/historic-columbus-venue-sees-concerts-axed-as-operator-pulls-out/)
- **Official website now (checked 2026-11-26):** columbusathenaeum.com returns a 301 redirect to theathenaeumtheatre.com, which redirects again to celebrityetc.com/athenaeum. That page is headed "The Athenaeum Theatre - Celebrity Etc Presents," lists 32 N 4th St, 614-286-2954, and states "**There are currently no upcoming events.**" It has no wedding, rental or private-event inquiry content — [Celebrity Etc / Athenaeum page](https://www.celebrityetc.com/athenaeum/)
- I found no news reports of couples' wedding events being cancelled. Coverage of cancellations is about concerts only — [Columbus Underground](https://columbusunderground.com/athenaeum-owner-cancels-all-shows-across-all-venues-we1/)

### Inferences
- The Athenaeum no longer runs its own events website and lists no events of any kind. Together with the pending sale, this points to **no reliable event operator for 2027**. A buyer could close at any time and change the building's use. A 2027 wedding booked now would carry high counterparty risk.
- The receiver presumably controls any bookings, but I found no evidence of a wedding sales team or a booking channel.

### Gaps
- I could not access the Franklin County Court of Common Pleas foreclosure docket or the Auditor's transfer records, so I cannot confirm whether a sale closed between April and November 2026. The receiver's name was not in any retrievable article (NBC4 returned 403 to direct fetch).
- I found no first-hand reports (WeddingWire/Zola reviews dated 2026) of weddings held at the Athenaeum in 2026.
