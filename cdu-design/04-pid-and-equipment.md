# 04 — P&ID, equipment list and packaging

Drawing: [04-pid.svg](04-pid.svg) (generated by [calc/make_pid.py](calc/make_pid.py)).

## Flow paths

**Server loop:** return from servers → V-101 → expansion tank tee → pump P-101 → relief and air vent → exchanger E-101 → filter F-101 → temperature, pressure and flow measurement → V-102 → supply to servers. A small bypass, V-105, links supply to return upstream of the isolation valves.

**Facility water:** supply → V-201 → strainer → fail-closed shut-off XV-201 → exchanger E-101 (counterflow) → flow meter → control valve TCV-201 → V-202 → return.

## Packaging

| Item | Decision |
|---|---|
| Envelope | 600 W × 800 D × 1000 H mm, on lockable castors with levelling feet |
| Mass | About 95 kg dry, 105 kg filled |
| Frame | Welded or bolted steel, powder coated; stainless drip tray |
| Drip tray | Full footprint, 40 mm deep, about 15 L; holds three times the cabinet's coolant volume |
| Wet zone | Lower 650 mm: pump and tank at the bottom, exchanger vertical on the rear wall, filter and strainer at the front |
| Dry zone | Upper 300 mm: control panel behind a sloped drip shield, cable entry from the top |
| Connections | Four 1" ports on the upper rear panel, with a bracket that carries hose weight |
| Front access | Door gives hand access to the filter cartridge, strainer screen, fill and drain coupling, sample port and all isolation valves |
| Pump change | Isolate, drain about 2 L to the tray coupling, undo two unions and four bolts, slide out the front |
| Pipework | 1" 316L tube with compression or orbital-welded fittings; no brass, zinc or aluminium in the server loop |
| Insulation | Closed-cell with sealed vapour barrier on all facility-side pipe and the exchanger |
| Labels | Flow arrows, fluid names, tag plates, relief setting, fill pressure |

## Wetted materials in the server loop

316/316L stainless steel, copper (exchanger braze only), EPDM, silicon carbide (pump seal), PTFE (valve seats). Any substitution needs coolant-supplier approval.
