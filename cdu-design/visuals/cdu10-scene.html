10 kW coolant distribution unit

CDU-10

The cooling unit beside the rack. It pumps coolant to the servers and hands their heat to the building's water. About the size of a small fridge.

How the heat moves

  1. The pump sends coolant out at 32 °C, through the filter, to the servers.
  2. Cold plates on the chips pass their heat into it; it comes back at about 37 °C.
  3. In the heat exchanger it gives that heat to the building's water, without mixing.
  4. The building water, now about 33 °C, carries the heat away to the cooling towers.
