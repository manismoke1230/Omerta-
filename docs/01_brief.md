# SAY LESS. — one-page brief

**Omertà Academy · collection film · 24.0 s · 60 fps · 1920 × 1080 and 1080 × 1920**

**What it is.** A proposed campaign film built entirely in code from the eight supplied garment images. No AI-generated video, no 3D, no stock footage. The garments are mockups; nothing here claims manufactured stock.

**The idea.** *Say less — let the clothes do the talking.* One matte burnt-orange stitch travels through the whole collection on a single path and a single progress value. It starts on dark knit, runs both raglan seams, underlines two worn chest prints without touching a letter, climbs a zip, turns onto a plaid line, crosses faded camo denim, rides the hero's chest band, and finally leads the eye to the O.

**How it holds together.**

- **One tableau, one camera.** All eight pieces lie in one arrangement. The camera visits each, then pulls back to show it was one collection all along. There is no hard cut anywhere in the 24 seconds.
- **Light does the editing.** Only the piece being shown is lit at full colour; neighbours sit dim and slightly soft, so a reveal is a change of light and focus rather than a cut.
- **Handoffs are physical.** A camo sleeve crosses the lens; the stitch crosses the foreground; the camera passes behind fabric; one zip becomes another along the same vertical line; a plaid line carries into denim; a thread sweep reveals the hero.
- **Portrait is its own composition.** The lineup is restacked into three columns over a hero band, lateral travel is shorter, and copy stays inside 120 / 180 / 300 px margins. It is never a crop of the landscape master.

**Look.** Charcoal stage `#171615`, burnt orange `#B45328`, washed cream `#E8DDCA`. Garment colours are untouched: a lit garment is displayed at exactly its photographed colour. Soft contact shadows, a restrained warm edge light, light grain.

**On-screen copy (the only three lines):** `SAY LESS.` · `OMERTÀ ACADEMY` · `EXPLORE THE COLLECTION.`

**Sound.** Original 100 BPM instrumental synthesised in code: fibre movement first, pulse at 2.40, weight at 7.20, percussion out at 16.80, warm chord at 19.20 with a tail under the end card. 29 sound-design cues, each tied to visible motion. Mastered to −14 LUFS, true peak −1.3 dBTP.

**Beat sheet.** Nine scenes on a 2.4 s bar (144 frames): see `review/storyboard_landscape.jpg` and `review/storyboard_portrait.jpg`.

2. **O mark.** No approved logo file was supplied. The end card uses a clean extraction of the O from the long-sleeve print, for approval.
