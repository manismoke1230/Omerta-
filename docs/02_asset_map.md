## Background removal

`sayless/matte.py`, settings per image in `assets/garments/manifest.json`. The backdrop is found by flooding in from the image border, so white fabric inside a garment is never keyed out. Edge colour is taken from just inside the silhouette, which removes the light fringe a white backdrop leaves on a dark stage. Check sheet: `review/mattes.jpg`.

Per-image notes:

- **White raglan.** White body on a white backdrop. The body is enclosed by the sleeves and collar, so it survives; the cast shadow under each cuff is removed with two small hand-placed regions.
- **Charcoal long sleeve.** Shoulders are almost the backdrop's grey. The silhouette is healed with a morphological close; there is a small soft step where the left shoulder meets the sleeve.
- **Bomber.** The photo itself crops the top of the hood, so the hood has a flat top edge.
- **Plaid jacket.** The wide studio shadow under the hem is peeled; both hanging zip pulls are kept.
- **Hoodie.** Soft photo edges; the edge is re-seated at the half-way point of the blur. Two see-through gaps between sleeve and body are kept open.
